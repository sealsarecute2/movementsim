Movement notes & sources +

Release W in the air. Hold A while turning left, or D while turning right. Smooth turns build speed; turning too sharply loses it. Counter-strafe to stop quickly.

CS-style uses 250 u/s running, 130 walking, 85 crouching, gravity 800, ground acceleration 5.5, friction 5.2, air acceleration 12 and a 30 u/s projected air cap. Both modes support hold-Space autojump. Bhop practice uses air acceleration 100 and no bhop speed limit or landing slowdown.

This is an independent Source-style approximation. It uses 256 Hz fixed steps, swept box collisions, step climbing and an approximate timed landing window. It does not run Source 2 or reproduce CS2’s exact subtick, jump-spam or landing curves. Current-style landing recovery replaces the old jump stamina model.

References: Valve’s Source SDK movement model ↗ · Valve’s January 2026 jump updates ↗. Constants above are simulator presets, not a verified dump of the latest CS2 build.
