## bhop_kitsune

- Map: **NoNimus**, credited as **Ghost1447951** on GameBanana.
- Original release: https://gamebanana.com/mods/126424
- Original download: https://gamebanana.com/dl/279756
- Author's license: **Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–NoDerivatives 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/
- `bhop_kitsune.zip` is the author's unmodified `kitsune_f.zip` release, including its original README and update note. MD5: `36f2da19ae5918b8b95d6d74a08fb405`.
- The browser route keeps six main stages, five stage portals, entry boosts, safe checkpoint pads, and the final White-stage exit. The bonus and ending lobby are omitted.
- `bhop_kitsune.json` is the prepared plain-block compatibility view. The author's map data retain their original license.

## bhop_lego

- Map: **Tony Montana (tmontana)**.
- Original LEGO textures: **Man Walker** (not rendered in the parkour view).
- Testing and feedback: **Nyastle**.
- Original release: https://gamebanana.com/mods/125406
- Original download: https://gamebanana.com/dl/294484
- Author's license: **Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–NoDerivatives 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/
- `bhop_lego.zip` is the author's **unmodified** `bhop_lego_3.zip` archive. Its original MD5 is `c916f2a56ef10e3a0a025c2c07d12e00`.
- The map-selection thumbnail is generated from the retained platform geometry.

The original archive remains unchanged for provenance and rebuilding. `bhop_lego.json` is a lightweight coordinate cache for a greybox parkour view: room scenery and bonus areas are omitted, and no original textures are rendered. The map data and original art retain the author's license and are not covered by any license for Movement Lab's code.

The parkour view uses plain blocks, ordered checkpoint platforms, section-specific fall resets, and automatic boost pads. It does not emulate original Source scenery or scripts. Prepared local geometry and run progress can be kept in this browser tab's session storage for reloads and are never uploaded.
